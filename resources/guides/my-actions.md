# My actions

One list of everything waiting on you, from every part of the business.

Expenses to approve, documents to review, purchases to sign off — they all
arrive here, rather than in five separate screens you have to remember to
check.

## Finding it

**My actions** in the main navigation, or go straight to `/actions`.

There is no permission to grant. If something has been assigned to you, you can
open it and answer it — being asked is itself the authority to act.

## What you see

Each item shows what it is, which workflow it belongs to, which step it is at,
and when it is due. Anything past its due date is marked **Overdue**.

The list is ordered with the most urgent first: items with a due date come
before those without, and the earliest due date comes first.

## Answering

Four buttons on each item:

- **Approve** — passes your part of the step.
- **Ask for changes** — sends it back to whoever submitted it, so they can fix
  something and resubmit.
- **Reject** — stops it permanently. You are asked to confirm, because there is
  no undo.
- **Add a note** — opens a box so your answer carries an explanation.

A note is worth adding on anything other than a straightforward approval. "Ask
for changes" without saying what to change simply produces the same submission
a second time.

## Things that surprise people

**Items leave the list the moment they are dealt with** — including when
somebody else answers. If a step only needs one approval and a colleague gets
there first, it disappears from your list too. That is not an error; the step is
done.

**Handing over removes it from your list and adds it to theirs.** You will not
see it again unless they hand it back.

**An empty list means nothing is waiting on you** — not that nothing is
happening. Work in progress that is currently with somebody else does not appear
here. To see the state of a particular record, open the record itself.
